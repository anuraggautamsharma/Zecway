# Outvo — Work AI Platform for the Enterprise

Outvo connects to every tool a company uses, understands all of its knowledge, and gives
every employee one place to **search, ask, and act** — with answers that always respect
who is allowed to see what.

> One search bar for the entire company. One assistant that actually knows your business.

## The one-paragraph pitch

Fortune 500 employees lose hours every week hunting for information scattered across
Google Workspace, Slack, Teams, Notion, Confluence, Jira, GitHub, and Salesforce.
Glean proved companies will pay $50+ per user per month to solve this — but it is
expensive, opaque, and slow to deploy. Outvo delivers the same core promise —
permissions-aware enterprise search plus an AI assistant grounded in company knowledge —
with a dramatically better, design-led experience, transparent pricing, and a deployment
that takes days instead of months.
