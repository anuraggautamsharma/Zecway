# Zecway — The Company Brain

Zecway aggregates everything a company produces — documents, PDFs, emails, chats,
spreadsheets, meeting notes — and converts it into a single **markdown knowledge graph**
that both humans and AI agents can natively read and work inside. On that foundation it
maps the company as a system (people, processes, tools) and gives executives
**evidence-backed answers in minutes** to the questions that used to take consultants
weeks.

> Zecway is the name. "The company brain" is the category we claim — always lowercase,
> always "the": a thing Zecway *is*, not a thing it's called.

## The one-paragraph pitch

When a leader has to act — restructure a team, keep or drop a vendor, find where
handoffs break — the answer is assembled today by consultants, internal analysts, or
asking around. All three are slow, partial, and stale the moment they're delivered.
Zecway already holds everything the company knows, mapped as a living system, with AI
agents that investigate like a consulting team and cite every claim. Same answers, in
minutes instead of weeks, at a fraction of the cost — and they stay current as the
company evolves.
